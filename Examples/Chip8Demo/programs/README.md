# CHIP-8 programs

From John Earnest's CHIP-8 Archive (https://github.com/JohnEarnest/chip8Archive), where everything is released under
Creative Commons 0 ("No Rights Reserved"). Credit to the authors anyway:

- `br8kout.ch8`: Br8kout by SharpenedSpoon (Octojam 1, 2014). Keys 7 and 9 slide the paddle. Archive options: tickrate 7, no quirks
  flags set (so shifts take VY, FX55/FX65 move I, sprites wrap, no VF reset, no display wait).
- `superpong.ch8`: Super Pong by offstatic (Octojam 8, 2021). Key 6 serves, keys 7 and 9 slide the paddle. Archive options: tickrate 30,
  every quirk flag off.
- `danm8ku.ch8`: Danm8ku by buffi (Octojam 2, 2015). "Survive 8 waves of exciting bullet patterns." Archive options: tickrate 1000,
  every quirk flag off.
